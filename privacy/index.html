Privacy

In effect from 18 November 2026

In short. We keep the information we need to teach you: your account, your lessons and payments at the school, your test results, and what you study in the IF App. The director, the manager and your own teachers can see it. Parents can see their child’s study report. We do not sell information and we do not show advertising.

1. Who we are

IF Academy (Intellect First) is a language school at Al-Farabi 19, Kostanay, Kazakhstan. This page covers the website intellectfirst.com, the IF App at intellectfirst.com/app/, the placement test, IF Live games and the school’s Telegram bot. The IF English app in the app stores has its own, separate policy.

2. What we keep

  • Your account: name, email address, phone number, and, if you give them, date of birth, city, a photo, your goals, and a parent’s name and phone number.
  • School records: your class, level, attendance, homework, teachers’ comments, and the months you have paid for.
  • Tests: your answers and results in the placement test and other tests. This includes what you write, and what you say in speaking tasks, which is turned into text.
  • Study in the IF App: the lessons, articles and games you finish, your scores, XP and streak, how many minutes you are active each day and at what time of day. This is recorded only when you are signed in, and the day-by-day record is kept for 120 days.
  • Messages: what you send to the school through the website, the Telegram bot or WhatsApp, so that we can answer.
  • Class games: in IF Live, the first name you type and your score; a teacher can save the results of a class game. Mafia Live and Mystery Live keep nothing after the game ends.
  • On your device: the site saves your settings, progress and notes in your browser so that pages work without a connection. We do not use advertising cookies.

3. Why we keep it

To give you access to your courses, place you at the right level, show you and your teachers your progress, organise lessons and payments, send lesson, homework and payment reminders, and answer your questions.

4. Who can see it

  • The director and the manager of IF Academy see the school’s records and every student’s study report.
  • Teachers see the records and study reports of students in their own classes only.
  • Parents: the school can send a parent a private link to their child’s study report. The link needs no password, shows study time, finished work and scores, and stops working after 90 days.
  • You can open your own study report from your profile at any time.

We do not publish this information, sell it, or give it to advertisers.

5. Services that work for us

Some information passes through companies that provide a service to the school. They may use it only to provide that service.

  • Netlify hosts the website, the accounts and the stored records, on servers in the United States.
  • Anthropic (the Claude AI) rates writing and speaking in tests and powers the site’s assistant. It receives the text of your answer.
  • ElevenLabs turns speech into text for speaking tasks and produces the voices you hear.
  • Ably carries the messages of live class games between phones.
  • Telegram and Green API (WhatsApp) deliver messages between you and the school.
  • ApiPay and Kaspi send and process tuition invoices. We never see your card details.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on the pages.

6. Children and teenagers

Many of our students are under 18. A parent or guardian arranges their place at the school, and can ask us at any time what we keep about their child, for a copy of it, or for it to be corrected or deleted.

7. How long we keep it

The day-by-day record of study in the IF App is kept for 120 days. Your account, results and school records are kept while you study with us or use your account. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and the information linked to it, except records of payments that we are required to keep.

8. Your choices

You can ask to see what we keep about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop the study report being shared with a parent. Write to us and we will reply as soon as we can.

9. Changes

If this page changes, we will change the date at the top and, for an important change, tell you in the IF App.
