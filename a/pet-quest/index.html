How to play

1. Spin a letter. Topic: .
2. The team with the bomb says a word from the topic that starts with the letter, then presses Pass.
3. No repeats, no long pauses. The fuse is secret: 20–45 seconds.
4. 💥 Team holding the bomb: −1★. The other team: +2★.

Describe the word. Don't say the forbidden ones!

    Crack the secret unit word, one letter at a time.

    Right letter = keep going. Wrong letter = lose a ❤ and the turn passes. Shout the whole word on your turn: right = +3★, wrong = −1★. Need a clue? The Russian hint costs 1★.
