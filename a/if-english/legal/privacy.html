Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

IF English is a vocabulary and games app made by IF Academy (Intellect First Academy), Kostanay, Kazakhstan (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking, and your learning progress stays on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings and learning progress (for example the units you opened, scores, streaks, IF Coins, the words you marked as known, your language and voice choices) in the app’s own storage on your phone or tablet. We do not receive this information. You can delete it at any time in Settings › Reset my progress or by deleting the app.

2. Subscriptions

If you buy IF English Premium, the payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your name, card or bank details. To check whether Premium is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app, your purchase records from Apple or Google (product, price, purchase and renewal dates, country) and basic device information (such as operating system and app version). This information is used only to provide and restore your subscription. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

3. Pronunciation audio

Words are read aloud by the text-to-speech voices built into your device. The app sends only the word to your device’s speech engine. Depending on your device settings, the speech engine may be provided by Apple, Google or another company under their own privacy terms.

4. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera input. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share personal information.

5. Children

Some courses are designed for young learners. The app does not ask children for personal information. Purchases are made through the parent’s or account holder’s App Store or Google Play account, which can require parental approval.

6. Contacting us

If you write to us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

7. Your rights

Because progress is stored only on your device, you control it directly. For subscription records you can contact us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete the data linked to your app identifier. You can also manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store or Google Play account.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
