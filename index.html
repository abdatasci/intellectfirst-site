How our teacher pathway works

Students at A2 level and below (teens and adults) first study with our local, Russian-speaking teachers for about 3–4 months. They explain grammar clearly in your own language and build a solid base. After that, students move on to lessons with a native speaker.

Young learners follow the same logic: Starters and Movers are taught by local teachers only. Flyers, KET and PET can be taught by local teachers or by native speakers.

Abdoulie Manneh

Founder & Director, IF Academy

Abdoulie Manneh is the founder of IF Academy and its native English-speaking teacher. Originally from The Gambia, he has taught for over 14 years in Africa, Russia and Kazakhstan, including corporate programmes for companies such as Lukoil and Rosatom. He holds degrees from universities in The Gambia, Taiwan and Russia, is TESOL-certified, and is completing a Doctor of Business Administration at Maqsut Narikbayev University.

Teaching philosophy:

Abdoulie believes a language is learned by using it. His lessons are built on real conversation, debate and games in small, personal classes. Having learned Russian and Arabic himself, he aims to make every lesson challenging and confidence-building. Each student should leave able to say something they couldn't say before.

07 — FAQs

Questions, answered

How do I get started?

Book a free placement test through Telegram or email — we'll assess your level and recommend the right class.

What levels do you teach?

Beginner (A1) through Upper-Intermediate (C1) for General English, plus Cambridge exam tracks (Starters, Movers, Flyers, KET, PET) for young learners.

Are classes online or in person?

Both — General English for the Russian market runs online; classes in Kazakhstan run in person across our four spaces (IF Business Lab, IF Hub, IF Studio and the IF Reading Zone).

Do you offer corporate or group training?

Yes — Corporate Training is quote-based and customised to your organisation, available online and offline.

Need to change a class time or teacher?

Contact the academy directly and we'll arrange it.

08 — About

Built on one conviction

Intellect First was founded on the conviction that language instruction should be judged the way any serious education is judged — by rigour, by outcomes, and by the calibre of the people delivering it.

Every class is led by qualified instructors, held to international standards, and built around small groups so that no student goes unheard. The result is an academy that operates less like a language centre and more like a proper school of thought.
